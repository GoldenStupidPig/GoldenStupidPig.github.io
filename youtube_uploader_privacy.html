Privacy Policy

YouTube Uploader — last updated: November 6, 2026. Back to the app page

Who uses this app

YouTube Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users. In this policy, "the owner's channel" means any YouTube channel that the owner owns or manages, including channels the owner adds or creates in the future; the app is never used for anyone else's channel.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their privacy status and scheduled publish time;
  • update the titles, descriptions, translations and captions of the owner's own videos;
  • create and update playlists on the owner's channel;
  • schedule live streams on the owner's channel and read and post messages in the owner's own live chat;
  • read the owner's channel information (channel name and ID) and the status of the channel's videos;
  • read comments on the owner's videos, reply to them and moderate them (only after the owner approves each reply);
  • read the channel's Super Chat events and the list of channel members and membership levels (to thank supporters);
  • read the owner's channel analytics, including revenue reports (for example views, watch time, traffic sources, countries, estimated revenue and RPM).

How the data is used

The data is used only to schedule and manage uploads on the owner's own channel and to review the channel's performance. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose. Viewer names that appear in comments, live chat, Super Chat or member lists are used only to reply to or thank those viewers on the owner's own channel and stream, and are never shared or sold.

Storage

All data stays on the owner's own computer: the sign-in token, upload records, schedules and analytics results are saved as local files. Nothing is stored on any server operated by this app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, transfer or share Google user data with anyone. The only external service it communicates with is Google's official APIs.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account > Linked apps. The owner can delete all local data by deleting the app's folders on their computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
